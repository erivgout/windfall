The recording dialog halves or doubles its monitor buffer. Half uses the whole number of milliseconds at or below half, and 5 ms stays 5 ms. Double stops at 100 ms. The buttons are hidden while dialog monitoring is off or armed tracks are in use, and a scale does not turn monitoring on. They stay disabled while recording or while the dialog is busy. Each mixer track's monitor buffer stays as it is. The dialog monitor level stays as it is.
