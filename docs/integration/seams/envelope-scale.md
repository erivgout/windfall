The sampler envelope section halves or doubles the attack, decay, and release.
Sustain stays as it is. Half of a 0 ms time stays 0. Double stops at 5000 ms for
attack and decay, and at 10000 ms for release. Times stay on tenths of a
millisecond. Pluck, Keys, Organ, and Pad stay as they are. The buttons are shown
only while the envelope is on, and a scale does not turn it on.
