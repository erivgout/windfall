The audio clip inspector halves or doubles each selected clip's fade in and
fade out independently. Half uses the whole number of ticks at or below half,
and a fade of 0 stays 0. Double stops at that clip's length. A side that would
not change is left out, and a clip with no changed sides is left out of the
updates.

Gain, pan, and pitch stay as they are. None, Short, Medium, and Long stay as
they are. Clip length stays as it is.
