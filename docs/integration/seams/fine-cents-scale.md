# Sampler fine cents scale

The sampler Sound section halves or doubles the fine-tune cents and keeps the
shown semitone. In tune stays in tune. Halve cents truncates toward zero, and
Double cents stops at 50 cents either way. At the highest semitone, a change
that would drop the cents is skipped.

The buttons follow the fine-tune presets and precede the sample gain presets.
Each click reads the latest stored tune and updates only tune. The tune Half
and Double buttons still scale the semitone. Root and gain stay as they are.
