The sampler sound section steps the fine tune from −50 cents toward +50 cents.
−50 cents stays −50 cents when moved previous. +50 cents stays +50 cents when
moved next. The shown semitone stays as it is. A cent value between two presets
moves to the neighboring preset. At the highest semitone, moving up from in
tune stays in tune. Root key and gain stay as they are.
