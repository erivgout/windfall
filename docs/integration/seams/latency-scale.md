The mixer latency panel halves or doubles one track's correction. Half moves
the correction toward no correction, and no correction stays at no correction.
Double stops at 1000 ms in either direction (-1000 through 1000 ms).

The buttons stay disabled while recording. The number field does not have to
be filled. None, 1 ms, 5 ms, and 10 ms stay as they are. This does not move
captured takes; recording offset is a different control.
