The effect mix menu offers Previous preset and Next preset to step one effect
from dry toward wet through the existing mix presets. Dry stays dry when moved
previous. Wet stays wet when moved next. A mix between two presets moves to the
neighboring preset in the chosen direction, and a mix within 0.001 of a preset
counts as that preset.

Each action reads the latest effect and updates only its mix. The effect's other
settings and other effect slots stay as they are.
