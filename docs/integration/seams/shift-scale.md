The channel timing section halves or doubles the shift. Half moves it toward the grid by the whole number of ticks at or inside half, so an early shift stays early or lands on the grid and a late shift stays late or lands on the grid. On-grid shift stays on the grid. Double stops at one quarter early or one quarter late. Swing mix and gate stay as they are. The 16th-early, on-grid, 16th-late, and 8th-late presets stay as they are.
