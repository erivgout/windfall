# Sampler tune scale

The sampler Sound section halves or doubles the tune semitone and keeps the
cents. Half moves the semitone toward unison. Unison stays in unison. Double
stops at 48 semitones in either direction, and a double that would drop the
cents does not apply.

Root key, sample gain, reverse, and cut stay as they are. The octave, fifth,
and unison presets stay as they are. The fine-tune presets stay as they are.
